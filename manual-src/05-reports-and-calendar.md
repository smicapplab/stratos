# 5. Reports & Calendar

Stratos isn't just for moving cards around; it provides powerful visualizations to help you understand your data.

## Calendar View

Tasks with due dates can be visualized on a Calendar. 

1. Navigate to any Board.
2. In the top right corner (next to Filters), click the **View Toggle** and select **Calendar**.
3. You will see all tasks plotted on their respective due dates. 
4. You can drag and drop a task to a different day to quickly reschedule it.

## Reports & Analytics

To get a bird's-eye view of your team's performance, use the Reports view.

1. Navigate to your Board.
2. Click the **View Toggle** and select **Reports**.
3. Here you can see:
   * **Task Distribution**: A pie chart showing how many tasks are in each stage (e.g., 5 in Todo, 3 In Progress, 10 Done).
   * **Assignee Workload**: See who has too much on their plate and who has capacity based on assigned Story Points.
   * **Burndown Chart**: A line graph plotting remaining work over time. It calculates velocity by summing the "Story Points" field of all unfinished tasks. As tasks are moved to "Done", the total points decrease, drawing a downward slope. If tasks don't have Story Points set, this chart will remain flat.
